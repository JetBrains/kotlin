- 2026-07-02 — [JSR-223 K2 bindings — backtick + delegated-property binding-name fix (Q14 refinement / G12)](iterations/2026-07-02_jsr223-binding-name-delegate-fix.md) — migration step 1 (JSR-223 K2 bindings) — **Q14 refined, new G12**: root-caused why the 2026-07-01e marker-encoding prototype needed to avoid backticks at all — a narrow K2 REPL/script-snippet parser bug where a backtick-quoted property with a hand-written `get()`/`set()` body fails to parse ("Property getter or setter expected") only when the same live-REPL-session snippet also calls `getBindings(...)` (an implicit-receiver call); an equivalent one-shot `.kts` compile of the identical source does not reproduce it.
